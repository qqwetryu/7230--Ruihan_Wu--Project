Week 11 Journal – Establishing Zones and Camera Rig Adjustment

This Week’s Focus
Building upon the spatial adjustments from Week 10, this week’s focus was to finalize and polish the zonal structure of the Inkroom prototype. I officially introduced and implemented a clear Saving Zone, expressed visually as a bookshelf on the user’s right. This area not only holds saved documents but also helps users anchor their understanding of “where” their work goes after interaction—supporting a familiar metaphor of file storage in a physical study room.

In parallel, I made several refinements to the Camera Rig (XR rig). Initially, the default viewing angle often caused important UI elements to fall outside the comfortable field of view. Through trial and error, I adjusted the camera height, field of view, and rotation angle, ensuring that key elements like the keyboard, writing pad, and bookshelf remained within reach and visible without head tilting or excessive movement.

Reflection
This week marked a turning point where the prototype started to feel more spatially coherent and cognitively aligned. The separation between zones not only organizes the space better but also supports task-based flow, allowing users to shift between writing, saving, and reflecting without losing context. Camera rig tuning, though tedious, paid off by drastically improving comfort and usability.
