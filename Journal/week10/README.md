Week 10 Journal – Iterative Refinement: Space and Scale

This Week’s Focus
During Week 10, my primary focus was continuing the structural and functional refinement of the prototype. After several rounds of internal testing and reflection, I identified key issues with the spatial scale and object positioning that hindered natural user interaction. For example, some interactive elements were too far from the user’s reach or appeared disproportionately large in the XR scene, affecting depth judgment and interaction precision.

To address this, I conducted a series of scale calibration tests, resizing key UI elements such as the writing pad, sticky notes, and bookshelf, while also adjusting the environment boundaries to allow more natural movement and access. These refinements aimed to create a more ergonomic and intuitive writing posture in the Inkroom setup.

Additionally, I began drafting concepts for gesture-based saving interactions, experimenting with object positions that would better support a future implementation of drag-and-drop document saving metaphors.

Reflection
Working on scale tuning helped me realize the complexity of spatial interaction design in XR. Unlike flat UI design, the 3D space introduces physicality and embodiment, meaning that even small misalignments in size or distance can cause user discomfort or confusion. This week reminded me of the importance of perspective-based iteration, especially when dealing with immersive systems.
